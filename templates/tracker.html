Understanding Your Cycle Calendar

This calendar helps you visualize your menstrual cycle and fertility window for the next 3 months. Here's what the different colors mean:

  • Red (Period Days): The days you can expect to be menstruating.
  • Light Blue (Fertile Window): The 5-6 days when you're most likely to conceive if you have unprotected sex.
  • Teal (Ovulation Day): The day when your ovary releases an egg. This is typically your most fertile day.

Note: This calendar provides an estimate based on average cycle patterns. Your actual cycle may vary. For the most accurate tracking, record your symptoms and period dates consistently.
